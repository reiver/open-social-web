About the Author

This article was written by Charles Iliya Krempeaux.
You can reach him on the Fediverse at @reiver@mastodon.social.

Charles Iliya Krempeaux has been an Open Social Web developer since the year 2021, and is both an active member of the Fediverse developer community, and the ATProto (ATmosphere) developer community. Charles was an early engineer (one of the 1st 4 senior engineers) at the social media company Hootsuite. He created the second hosting company on the Fediverse — SpaceHost — and as a result has a deep understanding of hosting Fediverse servers. He has worked on a number of ActivityPub server software and client apps. And, he implemented the ActivityPub protocol from scatch — in the Go programming language (golang). Charles is the organizer and founder of the Fediverse conference: FediCon. He created the 2nd custom feed generator for ATProto: RobinFeed. He created the 1st real-time search engine for the Fediverse and ATmosphere (ATProto): Now. He is a founding engineer at the ATProto social media company Blacksky.
